.**Project Overview**
    The Hostel Maintenance Management System is a system designed to improve the process of 
reporting, monitoring and managing maintenace problems within university halls/hostels.
    The platform will provide a way for students and staff living on campus to submit complaints on
their living conditions and help them track its progress. It will provide a way for the appropriate administrative bodies and maintenance personnel to review problems and update that their status.
    The project aims to make hostel maintenance reporting, monitoring and managing more organized 
and easy to access.

.**Problem Statement**
    Students in university halls will experience maintenance issues such as faulty electrical 
appliances, plumbing problems, damaged facilities, and so on. However, reporting these problems and ensuring they are resolved can be difficult due to the lack of an efficient reporting system.
    The existing process makes it difficult for students to submit complaints, receive feedback,
track the status of their requests, and know whether their reported issues are being addressed and at times, their complaints are completely overlooked. This leads to delays in resolving maintenance problems and inconvenience for students.
    This project aims to examine the challenges associated with maintenance reporting and follow-up 
within Babcock University halls and propose a centralized maintenance reporting system that would allow students to submit complaints, monitor progress, and improve communication between students and the university's maintenance personnel.

**Project Objectives**
The Project objectives are to:
-Develop an accessible platform that allows students to report hostel maintenance issues easily.
-Allow students to monitor the status and progress of their submitted maintenance requests.
-Enable authorized administrators to receive, review, and manage maintenance complaints efficiently.
-Tranfer the reported maintenance issues to the appropriate maintenance personnel for resolution.
-Maintain a centralized digital database of maintenance requests, including pending, ongoing, and resolved issues.
-Improve communication and feedback between students, hostel management, and maintenance personnel.
-Enhance the monitoring and management of hostel maintenance activities through organized records and status updates.

**Target Users**
**Students**
    Students residing in university hostels will be the primary users of the system. They will be
 able to report maintenance issues, provide descriptions and relevant details, and track the progress of their submitted requests.
**Hostel Administrators**
    Hostel administrators will be responsible for receiving, reviewing, monitoring, and managing 
maintenance complaints submitted by students. They will also coordinate the handling of reported issues and ensure that requests are directed to the appropriate personnel.
**Maintenance Personnel**
    Maintenance personnel will be responsible for handling assigned maintenance requests, updating
 the status of ongoing repairs, providing feedback, and recording completed maintenance work through the platform.

**Stakeholders**
**University Facilities Management**
    The University's Facilities Management Department will serve as its stakeholder. Its 
involvement  includes overseeing maintenance activities, coordinating personnel, and monitoring the resolution of reported issues.

**Research and Findings**
    The purpose of this research is to identify the challenges students face when reporting hostel 
maintenance problems at Babcock University and determine how a digital reporting system can improve the process.

 Primary Research

    A survey was conducted among 10 students of Babcock University to understand their experiences 
with hostel maintenance problems and the existing complaint-reporting process.

Secondary Research

    Secondary research involved reviewing Babcock University's hostel information, existing 
maintenance procedures and other hostel management systems. This helped identify how technology can improve maintenance reporting, complaint tracking and communication.

Survey Results

A total of 10 Babcock University students participated in the survey. The responses are presented below.

Survey Question| Yes| No
Have you experienced hostel maintenance problems?| 7 (70%)| 3 (30%)
Have you reported maintenance issues before?| 8 (80%)| 2 (20%)
Do you experience delays in repairs?| 8 (80%)| 2 (20%)
Would you prefer reporting complaints online?| 8 (80%)| 2 (20%)
Do you believe tracking complaint progress would be helpful?| 9 (90%)| 1 (10%)

 Research Findings

Based on the survey conducted, the following findings were identified:

1. 70% of the students have experienced maintenance problems in their hostels.
2. 80% have previously reported maintenance issues.
3. 80% experience delays in getting maintenance problems resolved.
4. 80% prefer reporting maintenance complaints through an online platform.
5. 90% believe that tracking the progress of maintenance complaints would be helpful.

These findings show that students need a more accessible and organized way to report and monitor hostel maintenance problems.

Problem Validation

    The survey results confirm the need for an improved hostel maintenance reporting process at 
Babcock University. The high percentage of students experiencing repair delays and the strong interest in online complaint reporting show the potential value of the proposed system

**Proposed Solution**

How the system will work:
1. A student reports a maintenance problem.
2. The system generates a complaint reference number.
3. The administrator reviews and assigns the complaint.
4. The maintenance worker attends to the problem.
5. The student can monitor the complaint until it is resolved.

**User Stories**
1. As a student, I want to create an account so that I can report maintenance problems.
2. As a Hall Administrator, I want to check the complaints of the students.
3. As a Hall Administrator, I want to contact the appropriate personnel to resolve the problems.
4. As a Maintenance Personnel, I want to show the progress of the ongoing repairs.
5. As a Maintenance Personnel, I want to notify the Hall Administrators of any complications that 
arise.
6. As a Maintenance Personnel, I want to notify students of completed repairs.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- User's must be authenticated
- User's name must be provided
- Maintenance Personnel job descriptions must be provided
- Self Image of Maintenance Personnel must be uploaded
- Invalid submissions must be rejected with appropriate error

**Initial Functional Requirements**

Functional requirements explain the different things the proposed system should be able to do:

1. Students should be able to create accounts and log in.
2. Students should be able to report maintenance problems online.
3. Users should be able to select the type of problem they are reporting.
4. Students should be able to describe problems and provide their hostel and room details.
5. Students should be able to upload pictures of damaged facilities.
6. The system should generate a unique number for every complaint.
7. Students should be able to check the progress of their complaints.
8. Administrators should be able to view and manage submitted complaints.
9. Administrators should be able to assign repair jobs to maintenance workers.
10. Maintenance workers should be able to update the progress of their work.
11. The system should keep records of previous complaints.
12. Completed maintenance requests should be marked as resolved.

**Initial Non-Functional Requirements**

Non-functional requirements explain the qualities and standards the proposed system should meet to work effectively:

1. Usability: The system should be simple and easy for everyone to understand and use.
2. Responsiveness: It should work properly on mobile phones, tablets and computers.
3. Security: Users' personal information should be protected.
4. Access Control: Only authorized users should have access to administrative functions.
5. Reliability: The system should work properly without frequent breakdowns.
6. Performance: Pages and submitted requests should load within a reasonable time.
7. Data Storage: Complaint records should be properly stored and protected from loss.
8. Compatibility: The system should work on popular browsers such as Chrome, Firefox and Microsoft Edge.
9. Maintainability: The system should be easy to update and improve when necessary.
10. Scalability: It should be able to accommodate more students and complaints as time goes on.
11. Backup: Important records should be backed up to prevent permanent data loss.
12. Availability: Students and staff should be able to access the system whenever they need it, except during maintenance or service interruptions.

**Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**

The first version will focus on the essential features required to report and monitor maintenance complaints.

Included Features

* User registration and login.
* Student complaint submission.
* Maintenance category selection.
* Hostel and room location details.
* Complaint reference numbers.
* Complaint status tracking.
* Administrator complaint dashboard.
* Complaint assignment.
* Maintenance status updates.

Features Outside the Initial MVP

* AI-powered complaint classification.
* SMS notification integration.
* Online payments.
* Native mobile application.

**Project Risks and Limitations**

While the Hostel Maintenance Management System is expected to make reporting and managing maintenance issues easier, there are some challenges that may affect how well the system works.

Some of the possible risks include:

* Poor internet connection: Some students may not always have access to a stable internet connection, which could make it difficult to use the platform when reporting problems.
* Difficulty adapting to the system: Some students or hostel staff may prefer the current method of reporting complaints and may take time to get used to a new digital platform.
* Delay in fixing reported problems: Even when complaints are submitted successfully, repairs may still take time due to the availability of maintenance workers, equipment or materials.
* Incomplete information: Students may sometimes provide insufficient details when reporting a problem, making it difficult for maintenance personnel to understand exactly what needs to be fixed.
* Security issues: Since the system will contain user information and maintenance records, there is a risk of unauthorized people gaining access if proper security measures are not put in place.
* Dependence on university cooperation: The successful use of the system may depend on the support and cooperation of hostel administrators and the relevant university departments.

To reduce these challenges, the system should be simple to use, provide clear instructions when submitting complaints and have proper security measures. There should also be good communication between students, administrators and maintenance personnel to ensure that reported problems are attended to.

**Conclusion**

The Hostel Maintenance Management System is a proposed solution that aims to make reporting and managing maintenance problems in Babcock University hostels easier and more organized.

With the system, students will have a more convenient way to report problems and check the progress of their complaints, while hostel administrators and maintenance personnel will be able to manage and monitor reported issues more effectively.

Although there may be some challenges during its implementation, proper planning, cooperation and feedback from users can help make the system useful and reliable.

Overall, the project is aimed at improving the hostel maintenance process and providing students with a better way to communicate their maintenance needs.
