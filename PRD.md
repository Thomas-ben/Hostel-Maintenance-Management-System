.**Project Overview**
    The Hostel Maintenance Management System is a system designed to improve the process of 
reporting, monitoring and managing maintenace problems within university halls/hostels.
    The platform will provide a way for students and staff living on campus to submit complaints on
their living conditions and help them track its progress. It will provide a way for the appropriate administrative bodies and maintenance personnel to review problems and update that their status.
    The project aims to make hostel maintenance reporting, monitoring and managing more organized 
and easy to access.

.**Problem Statement**
    Students in university halls will experience maintenance issues such as faulty electrical 
appliances, plumbing problems, damaged facilities, and so on. However, reporting these problems and ensuring they are resolved can be difficult due to the lack of an efficient reporting system.
    The existing process makes it difficult for students to submit complaints, receive feedback,
track the status of their requests, and know whether their reported issues are being addressed and at times, their complaints are completely overlooked. This leads to delays in resolving maintenance problems and inconvenience for students.
    This project aims to examine the challenges associated with maintenance reporting and follow-up 
within Babcock University halls and propose a centralized maintenance reporting system that would allow students to submit complaints, monitor progress, and improve communication between students and the university's maintenance personnel.

**Project Objectives**
The Project objectives are to:
-Develop an accessible platform that allows students to report hostel maintenance issues easily.
-Allow students to monitor the status and progress of their submitted maintenance requests.
-Enable authorized administrators to receive, review, and manage maintenance complaints efficiently.
-Tranfer the reported maintenance issues to the appropriate maintenance personnel for resolution.
-Maintain a centralized digital database of maintenance requests, including pending, ongoing, and resolved issues.
-Improve communication and feedback between students, hostel management, and maintenance personnel.
-Enhance the monitoring and management of hostel maintenance activities through organized records and status updates.

**Target Users**
**Students**
Students residing in university hostels will be the primary users of the system. They will be able to report maintenance issues, provide descriptions and relevant details, and track the progress of their submitted requests.
**Hostel Administrators**
Hostel administrators will be responsible for receiving, reviewing, monitoring, and managing maintenance complaints submitted by students. They will also coordinate the handling of reported issues and ensure that requests are directed to the appropriate personnel.
**Maintenance Personnel**
Maintenance personnel will be responsible for handling assigned maintenance requests, updating the status of ongoing repairs, providing feedback, and recording completed maintenance work through the platform.

**Stakeholders**
**University Facilities Management**
The University's Facilities Management Department will serve as its stakeholder. Its involvement  includes overseeing maintenance activities, coordinating personnel, and monitoring the resolution of reported issues.
